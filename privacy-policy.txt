# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 5, 2026

Thank you for using this application ("RuneLite").

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Information We Collect

The App does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information or personal data.

Specifically, the App does not:

* Create user accounts.
* Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses.
* Collect location data.
* Collect contacts, photos, or files.
* Collect device identifiers for analytics or advertising.
* Use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

## Data Storage

The App does not maintain any servers or databases that store user information.

Any settings or preferences you configure within the App are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to the developer.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services that collect user information.

If future versions of the App introduce third-party services or features that collect data, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

## Permissions

The App may request Android permissions solely to provide its intended functionality. Any data accessed through these permissions remains on your device unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this document.

## Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the App's Google Play listing.
